## Platform

## Users

Retail store cashiers, register supervisors, and shop clerks processing in-person sales, order management, customer selection, and cart operations.

## Product Purpose

POS Cart & Orders (TFA4) is a CodeIgniter 4 Point of Sale application featuring transactional cart state, order placement, customer linkage, and authenticated cashier workflows. Success means rapid product scanning, accurate sales tax calculations, and reliable order ledger recording.

## Positioning

TFA4 represents the culmination of the CodeIgniter 4 POS curriculum: integrating the catalog, customer database, user auth, and transactional checkout into a unified retail register.

## Operating Context

- **Environment**: Checkout registers, point-of-sale workstations, and mobile inventory tablets.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Cashier logs in to their register station.
  2. Cashier searches or selects items from the catalog.
  3. Items enter the persistent session cart with live subtotal, VAT calculation, and quantity controls.
  4. Cashier confirms customer details and tenders payment to record the order.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Backend**: CodeIgniter 4 with PHP 8.2+.
- **Database**: MySQL with relational tables for products, categories, customers, orders, order items, and users.
- **Cart & Orders**: Full transactional cart mechanics with stock checks.
- **Design Tokens**: Bright-Market artisan ledger system.

## Product Principles

1. **Tabular Calculations**: Fixed-width digits for prices, subtotals, and change calculations.
2. **Deterministic Cart**: No phantom items or lost line items during register navigation.
3. **High Contrast Receipts**: Thermal-ready and print-ready monospace transaction receipts.
